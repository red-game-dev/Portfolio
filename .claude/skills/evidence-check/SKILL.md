# Evidence check

Every fact on the site must come from somewhere Red can stand behind. Before you write one, answer:

1. Where does it come from? One of: something the site already says, Red's own words in this conversation, his CV, a public source you can link, or the repository itself (git history, test counts).
2. Is it a floor? Counts are written as floors with a plus ("320+ commits"), never rounded up, and never more precise than the source.
3. Is the measure named? "sessions a month", "users in 167 countries", "years of real use".
4. Could it identify someone who did not agree to be named? Recommendations are attributed by role and company only.

If the only source is memory, yours or a guess, do not write it. Ask Red with a recommendation instead.

Skill years are never typed: they come from the forge, from roles and projects that list the skill, with floors for release dates and first use (see "Section ids and scroll-spy" in CLAUDE.md). To change a year, change the evidence, not the number.
